# Transient Bol read recovery — visual contract

The exact current Ampere scanner must never present readiness while unhealthy or stale shipment data disables the scan input. In the paused state, the health bar exposes Retry, the scanner heading says scanning is paused, the hint tells the operator to retry before scanning, the flag remains WAIT, the current status states that complete data is required, and an empty decision panel states that shipment data is incomplete. A real prior package decision remains visible.

After a complete healthy snapshot commits, the same artifact must return to the normal ready heading, scan hint, READY flag, focused input, and idle or last real decision without layout movement that breaks the operational flow.

At 1440px desktop and 390px mobile widths, paused and recovered-ready states must remain readable, contained without page-level horizontal overflow, and in the existing attention order. The intentional failed request may produce its matching browser network-console entry; no JavaScript page error or unrelated console error is acceptable. No HTTP status, retry attempt, raw upstream detail, credential, Vercel/Turso internal, or verification narration may appear in visible or assistive product output.
