# Client Bol tab — visual contract

The authenticated production scan station must present the two approved fixed sources as an explicit account choice immediately before scanning. Bankhoes remains selected first. Selecting Client Bol must create a visibly distinct operational context without exposing account identifiers or secrets.

The selected account tab controls the source-qualified scanner context, shipment table, order worklists, progress metrics, and source-qualified cancelled packages. Source-unqualified STOP history stays visible as shared safety review. The scanner remains the next actionable control after selecting a tab, and a source switch is unavailable while a scan decision is resolving.

At desktop and mobile widths, the account tabs must remain legible, the Client Bol selected state must be unambiguous, the scanner must remain operable, and the page must not acquire horizontal overflow. The released page must have no console errors.
